Eco Tips
  • 🧴

    Switch to refillables. Shampoo bars and soap blocks eliminate most bathroom PET bottles — saving ~3kg of plastic yearly per person.

  • 🛍

    Carry a cloth bag. A single reusable bag replaces ~500 LDPE bags over its lifetime. Keep one folded in your pocket.

  • 🥤

    Ditch the straw. PP straws are too small to recycle in most facilities. Metal or bamboo alternatives last years.

  • 🧊

    Freeze & batch. Buying in bulk reduces packaging waste by up to 60% compared to single-serving plastics.

  • 📦

    Choose cardboard over plastic. When given a choice, opt for paper or glass packaging — both have higher recycling rates.
